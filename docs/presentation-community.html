What Lee County can do next

The planner’s brief becomes a community brief.

The agent helps the disaster relief planning team sequence outreach instead of sending a generic alert to everyone.

01

Prioritize

Start with high-stranded zones: Pine Island / Matlacha, Central Fort Myers, North Cape Coral, and Sanibel.

02

Mobilize

Find nearby libraries, community centers, nonprofits, churches, volunteer centers, and neighborhood Facebook groups.

03

Pair

Ask those trusted places to recruit peers and invite residents into Neighbor Lift before hurricane season.

04

Act

Turn the analysis into LeeTran staging, medical-needs assignments, check-ins, and redundant people + destinations.

Example: Pine Island / Matlacha becomes more than a red tract on a map—it becomes a paratransit plan staged at Pine Island Fire Station #1, plus a local peer network.
Grounding + honest limits

Use Maps for the place. Use people for the unknown.

Google Maps can help confirm a registered shelter still exists, find a current place, and support routing. It cannot prove a shelter is open, accessible, or medically appropriate.

Unrecorded is not no.
When Maps or the source data cannot confirm the last minority of shelters, the agent creates a human validation task for a trusted Lee County partner—then the plan keeps a backup.
