# No One Left Behind · Lee County · 3-minute pitch

## Purpose

This is the short recording cut of the Lee County disaster-relief planning pitch. It compresses the six-slide handoff into three visual beats with less copy and an automatically looping agent conversation.

## Story

1. **Human stake + county signal** — “No one left behind,” the Hurricane Ian age statistic, and four Lee County numbers: 3,840 no-vehicle households, 1,420 power-dependent residents, and 87.5% unrecorded shelter accessibility.
2. **Agent to action** — the planner asks about risk, receives a Lee County answer, asks where to find community groups, and receives the outreach path. The four actions are numbered: 1 Prioritize, 2 Mobilize, 3 Pair, 4 Act.
3. **Community reliability** — Google Maps grounds places and routes; volunteers validate the unknown; Neighbor Lift carries the resident plan. The live app screenshots and Lee County CTA close the pitch.

## Final decision

Use `presentation-community-v2.html` for a roughly three-minute narrated recording. Keep the six-slide `presentation-community.html` for handoff and discussion. Both decks use the same warm visual system and the same Lee County evidence.
