What the agent can answer

“Which county should we work on first, and why?”
“What is the best verified destination for this tract?”
“Which facts are known, which are unrecorded, and who should verify them?”
“Who has no peer coverage, and what happens if the first plan fails?”
The product never exposes inferred individual vulnerability. It plans at tract/building-cluster level and only shows building-level peer work for voluntary or synthetic demo records.

Why this stands out

The map is not a dashboard. It is a conversation surface: click a priority area, ask why, verify a destination, assign a human action, and see the fallback if it fails. The agent knows when to query BigQuery, when to call Maps, and when to say “unrecorded—someone must check.”

Prototype language: evidence, uncertainty, consent, and ownership. No individual names or diagnoses in the public-data workflow.
